# Emergency fund

An emergency fund is money kept aside for unexpected costs such as a medical bill or a sudden loss of income. It is meant to be easy to reach, so it is usually kept in a safe, accessible account and not in risky investments.

A common rule of thumb is to keep three to six months of essential expenses. The right amount varies with job stability, family situation and existing insurance, so treat it as a starting point and not a rule.

FinGuru's Next Best Action uses three months of expenses as its target. That is a simple assumption chosen for this project. Source: written for FinGuru as general education.
