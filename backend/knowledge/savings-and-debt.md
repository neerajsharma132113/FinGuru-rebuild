# Savings rate and EMI share of income

The savings rate is the part of your income left after spending, shown as a percentage. For example, earning 80,000 and spending 60,000 leaves 20,000, which is a 25 percent savings rate.

The EMI share of income is your total monthly loan EMIs divided by your monthly income. A high share leaves little room for surprises. Lenders and planners often use a cap around 40 percent as a rough guide, but limits differ between lenders and situations.

FinGuru uses 40 percent as its warning level. That figure is an assumption for this project, not an official rule. Source: written for FinGuru as general education.
