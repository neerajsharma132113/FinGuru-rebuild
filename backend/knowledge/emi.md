# What is an EMI

An EMI (equated monthly instalment) is the fixed amount you pay every month to repay a loan. Each EMI contains two parts: interest on the loan balance that is still unpaid, and a repayment of part of the principal.

In the early months most of the EMI goes to interest. As the balance falls, the interest part shrinks and the principal part grows. This is why paying extra early reduces the total interest more than paying extra late.

A longer tenure lowers the monthly EMI but raises the total interest paid. A shorter tenure does the opposite. Source: written for FinGuru as general education. It is not advice about any specific loan.
